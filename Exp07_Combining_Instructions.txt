Experiment 7: Combining Automata
Open Exp07A and Exp07B in separate JFLAP windows.
Use Convert > Combine Automata (if available in your JFLAP version).
The sample demonstrates intersection using complements and union.
These two source DFAs recognize:
  A: strings with an odd number of a's
  B: strings with no "ab" substring
The product-state construction can also be used to form their intersection.
